# Prompts

Two prompts for turning a recording into a polished demo video. Pick one:

- `demo-video-faceless.md` — no one on camera. Works from the stills, the step log and the transcript alone; no video tool needed.
- `demo-video-with-face.md` — an edit of the webcam recording: the presenter's own voice is the soundtrack and their face is overlaid from `camera.webm` (recorded with **Record my camera** on). No synthesized voice. Needs a video tool behind the AI.
